# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads define quality standards, design test strategies, and validate that products meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria with Product and Project teams
- Design and maintain test plans, test cases, and automated testing frameworks
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Participate in acceptance validation and sign-off

### Goals
- Ensure products meet quality standards and customer expectations
- Reduce defect escape rate through comprehensive test coverage
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA sync with development and product leads
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria refinement
- Quality metrics and trend reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to define testability requirements and automate test coverage
- Partners with **Product Managers** to clarify acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Supports **Project Managers** by identifying quality risks and tracking test readiness
- Collaborates with **Technical Leads** on test infrastructure and automation strategy

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical strategy, review designs, manage technical debt, and ensure solutions are scalable and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Coach developers on best practices and design patterns
- Manage technical debt and refactoring efforts
- Ensure alignment with organizational technical standards

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce long-term technical risk and development friction
- Build team capability through mentoring and guidance

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture discussions
- Technical spike assessments
- Technical debt backlog refinement

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Mentors and guides **Developers** through code reviews and design discussions
- Works with **Product Managers** to balance feature requests with technical constraints
- Advises **Project Managers** on technical complexity, effort estimates, and risk mitigation
- Supports **QA/Testing Leads** in designing testable architectures and automated test infrastructure

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, schedule release windows, manage release communications, and oversee production rollouts.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule release windows and coordinate with stakeholders
- Prepare and review release notes and deployment runbooks
- Coordinate staging verification and smoke tests
- Manage deployment checklists and verify production readiness
- Communicate release status and rollback decisions

### Goals
- Execute releases on schedule with minimal risk and downtime
- Ensure smooth stakeholder and user communication
- Reduce time-to-value and deployment cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and scheduling meetings
- Pre-release readiness reviews
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinates with **Developers** and **QA/Testing Leads** on release readiness and deployment verification
- Works with **Product Managers** to communicate feature releases and manage customer expectations
- Partners with **Project Managers** on release timelines and stakeholder coordination
- Ensures **Technical Leads** have reviewed rollback procedures and deployment risks

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads review for security risks, ensure compliance with organizational standards, and guide secure development practices across all projects.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Define security standards, practices, and tooling for the organization
- Conduct security audits and threat assessments
- Provide security guidance and training to development teams
- Manage security incident response and post-incident reviews

### Goals
- Reduce security risk and protect customer data
- Build security awareness and best practices into the development culture
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational requirements

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat modeling sessions
- Security guidance in pull request reviews
- Security incident communications and post-mortems

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Reviews **Developer** code and designs for security vulnerabilities
- Advises **Product Managers** on security implications of feature decisions
- Works with **Project Managers** on security risk management and compliance tracking
- Collaborates with **Technical Leads** on secure architecture and security infrastructure
- Coordinates with **QA/Testing Leads** on security testing and vulnerability validation

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve scope and resource decisions, and ensure project alignment with organizational goals and strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success criteria for projects
- Provide subject matter expertise and domain knowledge
- Review and approve project scope, budget, and resources
- Resolve conflicts and make executive decisions when needed
- Receive regular project status updates and escalations

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Minimize wasted effort on low-value initiatives
- Maintain organizational alignment and strategic focus

### Typical Communication
- Project approvals and go/no-go decisions
- Monthly or quarterly stakeholder briefings
- Escalations for scope, resource, or schedule conflicts

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Sets strategic direction for **Product Managers** through business goals
- Reviews **Project Manager** plans and resource decisions
- Receives escalations from **Project Managers** on critical issues
- Provides business context to help **Technical Leads** make trade-off decisions
- Approves significant scope changes that impact **Developers** and other team members
